## Author doctrine — property specs, prices, underwriting (July 2026 notes; supersedes all prior bands)
- **Minimum property spec: 3 bed / 2 bath / 1,500 sq ft. Recommended: 4 bed / 3 bath / 2,000+ sq ft.** Jeff NEVER suggests 2 bed / 1 bath. Outlier illustration (label it an outlier): Jeff recently saw a cabin that sleeps 30 charging $2,000 a night — that's where rural homes can really shine, but most deals are not that.
