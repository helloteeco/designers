## Day 1 — Put it on Amazon

- ☐ kdp.amazon.com → sign in → complete the tax interview with Teeco's EIN and bank account.
- ☐ (Recommended) Buy the Bowker ISBN 10-pack at myidentifiers.com ($295) so "Teeco" is publisher of record. Skip = free KDP ISBN, "Independently published."
- ☐ **Ebook:** Create → Kindle eBook → paste title, subtitle, description, 7 keywords, 3 categories from AMAZON_LISTING_KIT.md → answer YES to the AI-content question → upload `book/Vacation Rentals That Pay You - Kindle.epub` + `book/... - Ebook Cover.jpg` → $7.99, enroll in KDP Select → Publish.
- ☐ **Paperback:** Create → Paperback → same details → upload `book/... - Print Interior 6x9.pdf` → upload `book/... - Print Cover Wrap.pdf` (already sized: 263 pages, white paper, 0.5923" spine, bleed included) → run KDP's previewer → **order the proof copy** (~$5).
- ☐ Claim Author Central (author.amazon.com): photo, bio, link the book.
- ☐ Schedule the ebook's **Kindle Countdown Deal at $0.99** for your launch week.

## Week 1 — While the proof ships

- ☐ **Read your book, cover to cover** (the print PDF). You are the last word on whether it sounds like you. KDP accepts corrected files anytime without losing reviews.
- ☐ Verify the three facts only you know: the $80K best flip and ~$200K PharmD cost (Chapter 1), and the West Virginia purchase timing (Chapter 2).
- ☐ Get **signed releases** from Myla, Ryu, Steph & Kevin, and Diane & Jon covering their names, numbers, property names, and marketing use. (This one no audit can replace; a one-page release each is enough.)
- ☐ Build the **substantiation file**: platform statements and closing docs behind every printed number, kept in one folder.
- ☐ Put the **Bonus Pack live at teeco.co/bookbonus**: upload the whole `bonus-pack-upload/` folder (18 PDFs + the calculator) behind an email-capture form. Add the items only you can supply (`BONUS_PACK_README.md` lists them: your deal-analysis video, current 0% card list, course links, design-team contact).
- ☐ Rename the mini course so it doesn't carry "Airbnb."
- ☐ Proof copy arrives → check cover alignment, spine, margins, a few tables → approve. Publish hardcover after: same interior PDF; for the cover, download the 6×9 / 263-page hardcover template from kdp.amazon.com/cover-calculator and place the existing wrap art onto it.

## Weeks 1–6 — The launch machine (details in LAUNCH_PLAYBOOK.md)

- ☐ Pick launch day (~6 weeks out). Everything back-plans from it.
- ☐ Start a fresh Claude session with the playbook's context prompt (page 1). That session is your co-pilot for everything below.
- ☐ Recruit the launch team (goal 100–250) with the DM scripts; deliver ARCs via BookFunnel; send the 3-touch email sequence.
- ☐ Book 5–15 podcast slots with the pitch template.
- ☐ Post 3–5x/week from the 30 content hooks; document the journey (proof copy unboxing, cover poll, countdown).
- ☐ Build the launch-week bonus stack page (buy → forward receipt → bonuses). Bonuses for BUYING only, never for reviews.
- ☐ Prepare the "RURAL in 60 minutes" livestream from the 7-beat outline.

## Launch week

- ☐ Day 1: Countdown Deal live at $0.99 → email + IG announcement → midday livestream → launch-team go signal.
- ☐ Days 2–7: one nudge per day (rank screenshots, first reviews, student stories); podcasts drop; repost every buyer photo.
- ☐ Watch for the orange **#1 Best Seller badge** in your categories; screenshot it the minute it appears.

## After

- ☐ Raise the ebook to $7.99–$9.99 when the Countdown ends.
- ☐ Turn on Amazon Ads: 3 campaigns, $10–20/day, kill/scale every Friday (structure in the playbook).
- ☐ Record the audiobook (self-narrated) under Audible's new 50% exclusive model.
- ☐ One podcast a week, forever. Reply to reader emails within 48 hours.
- ☐ Day 90: KU renewal decision, category and price review.

## The definition of done — what counts as a 100% successful launch

Check every box above, and then these are the outcomes that mean it worked. All numbers are the playbook's targets.

- ☐ All three formats live on Amazon (ebook, paperback, hardcover), linked on one page, under Author Central with your photo and bio.
- ☐ teeco.co/bookbonus live and collecting emails before launch day.
- ☐ Launch team of 100–250 recruited and fired on launch day.
- ☐ Launch week: Countdown Deal ran at $0.99, one nudge per day, livestream delivered.
- ☐ The orange **#1 Best Seller badge** captured in at least one category (screenshot it).
- ☐ 25+ reviews within 30 days of launch (the launch team plus the book's own review-ask page do this).
- ☐ 5+ podcast interviews recorded or booked.
- ☐ After the Countdown: price raised, Amazon Ads running, and one podcast a week on the calendar.

If every box on this page is checked, the launch was a success by any indie-publishing standard — and the book becomes a durable asset that sells while you sleep.
