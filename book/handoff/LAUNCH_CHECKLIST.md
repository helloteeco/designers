- ☐ Get **signed releases** from Myla, Ryu, Steph & Kevin, and Diane & Jon covering their names, numbers, property names, and marketing use. (This one no audit can replace; a one-page release each is enough.)

## Launch week

- ☐ Day 1: Countdown Deal live at $0.99 → email + IG announcement → midday livestream → launch-team go signal.
- ☐ Days 2–7: one nudge per day (rank screenshots, first reviews, student stories); podcasts drop; repost every buyer photo.
- ☐ Watch for the orange **#1 Best Seller badge** in your categories; screenshot it the minute it appears.

## After

- ☐ Raise the ebook to $7.99–$9.99 when the Countdown ends.
- ☐ Turn on Amazon Ads: 3 campaigns, $10–20/day, kill/scale every Friday (structure in the playbook).
- ☐ Record the audiobook (self-narrated) under Audible's new 50% exclusive model.
- ☐ One podcast a week, forever. Reply to reader emails within 48 hours.
- ☐ Day 90: KU renewal decision, category and price review.

## The definition of done — what counts as a 100% successful launch

Check every box above, and then these are the outcomes that mean it worked. All numbers are the playbook's targets.

- ☐ All three formats live on Amazon (ebook, paperback, hardcover), linked on one page, under Author Central with your photo and bio.
- ☐ teeco.co/bookbonus live and collecting emails before launch day.
- ☐ Launch team of 100–250 recruited and fired on launch day.
- ☐ Launch week: Countdown Deal ran at $0.99, one nudge per day, livestream delivered.
- ☐ The orange **#1 Best Seller badge** captured in at least one category (screenshot it).
- ☐ 25+ reviews within 30 days of launch (the launch team plus the book's own review-ask page do this).
- ☐ 5+ podcast interviews recorded or booked.
- ☐ After the Countdown: price raised, Amazon Ads running, and one podcast a week on the calendar.

If every box on this page is checked, the launch was a success by any indie-publishing standard — and the book becomes a durable asset that sells while you sleep.
