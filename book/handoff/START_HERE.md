## The title decision (make it once, tonight)

The book is identical inside all three packages; only the title, subtitle, running heads, and cover differ.
- **Package 1 (recommended): Vacation Rentals That Pay You.** Most relatable, trademark-safe, keywords in the title.
- **Package 2: Booked Anywhere.** Best brand; needs its subtitle to explain itself.
- **Package 3: AirBNB Anywhere.** Violates Airbnb's published trademark guidelines for product names. Use ONLY if your attorney signs off.
Fastest tiebreak: post the side-by-side cover image to your Instagram story tonight ("which would you buy?") and let your audience pick.
