# LAUNCH PLAYBOOK
## Vacation Rentals That Pay You, by Dr. Jeff Chheuy

**Who this is for.** A two-person team: Jeff, plus a fresh Claude session in the Cowork app. The Claude session that wrote this document is gone. Assume the Claude reading this knows nothing. Everything needed to run the launch is in this file.

**How to use it.** Jeff: at the start of each week, open a Claude session, paste the Context Prompt from Section 1, then paste that week's Claude prompts from Section 2. Do your own checkbox list. Check the week's one metric on Sunday. That is the whole operating system.

**Source of truth.** The strategy and all Amazon numbers here come from LAUNCH_PLAN.md (verified July 2026). Re-check royalty rates, printing costs, and promo rules in the KDP dashboard the week you publish. Story numbers come from the manuscript and are exact; never round them in public copy ($83,937.81 stays $83,937.81).

**The five facts that drive everything:**
1. The book is a funnel, not a product. Its job is to put teeco.co/bookbonus in thousands of pockets. Royalties are a bonus.
2. Launch week ebook price: $0.99 via Kindle Countdown Deal (keeps the 70% royalty). Real price $7.99 after.
3. Goal that matters: the orange #1 Best Seller badge in 2 to 3 mid-size categories, plus 50 to 100 reviews in month one. That takes roughly 10 to 50 sales/day for a few days in well-chosen categories.
4. Amazon review rules: free advance copies plus a request for an honest review is allowed. Anything of value exchanged for a review is banned. Launch bonuses are for buying, never for reviewing.
5. Budget: $2,000 to $4,000 total. Ads $10 to $20/day, target $0.40 or less per click.

### Amazon Ads: starter structure (turn on Week 7, Sponsored Products only)

- Daily budget: $3 or $6. Bids start $0.30, cap $0.40.
- Starter keywords (exact): `airbnb investing`, `short term rental investing`, `vacation rental investing`, `how to start an airbnb`, `airbnb for beginners`, `short term rental book`, `vacation rental business`, `passive income real estate`, `str investing`, `buying a vacation rental`, `rental property investing book`, `airbnb hosting book`.
- Have Claude expand this to 25 (Week 7 prompt in Section 2).

**Money rules.**
- Total $10/day to start; only scale toward $20/day after a campaign proves it converts.
- Target $0.40 or less per click (book CPCs run $0.15 to $0.60; business nonfiction sits high).
- Breakeven context: paperback margin is about $6.04 at $16.99 and ebook nets about $5.44 at $7.99, so roughly 14 clicks at $0.40 per sale is breakeven. The funnel means break-even ads are acceptable: the Bonus Pack captures the email either way.

**Weekly kill/scale rules (every Friday, 15 minutes).**
1. Download the search-term report for the trailing 2 weeks.
2. KILL: any search term with 10+ clicks and 0 orders becomes a negative exact in its campaign.
3. KILL: any keyword averaging over $0.40 CPC with no orders for two straight weeks gets paused.
4. SCALE: any search term with 1+ orders and ACOS at or under 70% gets added as an exact keyword (if it came from the auto campaign, add it to the matching exact campaign and negative it in auto), and its bid goes up 10%.
5. Shift budget toward the campaign with the lowest cost per order. Never exceed the daily cap you set for the month.
