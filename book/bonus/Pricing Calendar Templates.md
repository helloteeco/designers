# Pricing Calendar Templates

Pricing is your profit lever, it works from anywhere, and it is never set-it-and-forget-it. The six rules live in your pricing tool (we use PriceLabs); these templates are the calendar discipline that keeps them tuned. Emergencies are the loud failure. Rates drifting while nobody's watching are the quiet one.

Pair this with the Pricing SOP in this pack. One line from it applies to everything below: the weekly review is the one job you never delegate.

## Template 2 — The Weekly 15-Minute Calendar Review

Once a week, every other week at minimum. Open the PriceLabs Market Dashboard and run the five checks. Log one row per review so drift has nowhere to hide.

The five checks:

1. **Minutes 1–5, scan the market.** What are comparable listings actually getting per night? Where is the season heading? How does occupancy look? How far out are guests booking?
2. **Minutes 5–8, check the six rules.** If the market has moved and a rule hasn't, adjust it.
3. **Minutes 8–11, look ahead.** Holidays or local events coming? Boost 20–50% and stretch minimums. Stranded 1–2 night gaps? Discount them to fill.
4. **Minutes 11–13, confirm calendars are synced.** Hospitable syncs Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com in real time; Furnished Finder requires manually blocking dates, so keep a shared doc with your team. A double-booking is a 1-star review waiting to happen.
5. **Minutes 13–15, base-price check.** On cadence per Template 4.

## Template 4 — The Cadence Calendar

How often each job happens, by phase:

| Job | Launch phase (first months) | Stabilized (reviews grown) |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar review (Template 2) | Weekly, and watch trends closely | Weekly; every other week at minimum |
| Base price and rules update | Every 2–4 weeks until things stabilize | Revisit every 30–60 days |
| Pricing Ladder step-ups | Weeks 1–2: full 20% below market. Weeks 3–4: 10% below. Month 2: market rate. Months 3–6: 5–10% above once your rating is 4.8+ | Ladder complete; optimize for profit |
| Seasonal calendar (Template 3) | Build it at launch | Refresh once a year, review each quarter |
| Furnished Finder manual blocks | Every booking, same day, in the shared doc | Same. No exceptions. |

Who does what: PriceLabs handles most of it, and a VA can glance at the dashboard weekly and confirm the recommendations make sense. You step in manually for holidays and events. The review itself stays yours. Delegation is not abdication.
