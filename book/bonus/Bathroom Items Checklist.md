# Bathroom Items Checklist

Two rules run the bathrooms. First, white towels and sheets only: easy to bleach, consistent everywhere, and stains don't survive. Second, stock to your sleeper count, not to the bathroom count. Make it feel intentional, not bare: counter staged with a tray, soaps, and a plant, plus the spa touches (rolled towels, bath salts, candles).

Counts marked [MAX GUESTS] mean your maximum occupancy. Repeat the per-bathroom items for every bathroom in the home.
