# Kitchen Items Checklist

One rule drives every count on this page: you can't sleep twelve and seat four. Twelve sleepers means 12 dining chairs, 12 place settings, and 12 mugs. Write your max occupancy at the top and stock every guest-facing category to that number. Where a count says [MAX GUESTS], that's your number.

Aim for clean and functional, with just enough character: spotless counters with one or two styled touches, like a coffee station, a plant, or a cookbook. Buy like a hotel; if Hilton uses it, it survives guests.
