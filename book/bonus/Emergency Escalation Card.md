# Emergency Escalation Card

Print this. Pin it in Slack. Put it in your VA's hands. A protocol beats panic, every time. Emergencies happen: burst pipes, power outages, locked-out guests, mice. What matters is how fast and how calmly your system responds.

## The Escalation Matrix

A clear decision tree prevents panic and keeps things moving fast.

| Tier | Who acts | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| $0–$50 issues | VA handles without asking | WiFi reset, power reset, small supplies, guest lockout (issue a fresh smart-lock code), basic communication |
| $50–$200 issues | VA calls for approval, then dispatches | Minor repairs, furniture moves, supply runs, quick plumbing fixes |
| $200+ issues | VA calls immediately, owner decides | Burst pipes, HVAC failure, roof leak, structural damage, major electrical work |
| Urgent and guest upset | Immediate owner call | Never handle guest conflict escalation through a VA. Own the conversation. |

**One note on "calls immediately," since that phrase worries people.** The VA stabilizes the guest first: acknowledge the message, dispatch the standby vendor from the pre-approved list, offer the playbook remedy. You get woken only when a decision can't wait until morning. Spending limits and playbooks keep the midnight call rare, not impossible.

This matrix removes decision fatigue and lets your team act without you.

## First Reply to the Guest

Reply quickly even if you don't have the fix yet:

> Thanks for flagging this, we're on it. I've messaged our repair team and will update you soon.

Then ask for photos:

> Can you send a quick photo so we can get the right person out to fix it?

Photos prevent exaggeration and give your team context.

## Smart-Device Early Warning

Use smart devices as your early warning system on a property you may not visit for months.

| Device | What it warns you about |
|---|---|
| Minut noise monitor | Temperature drops before pipes freeze; flags smoking or parties before they become damage (no audio recorded, real-time alerts) |
| Ring exterior camera | Shows you what's actually happening instead of what you fear is happening; catch parties early, confirm contractors actually showed up |
| Smart lock (Schlage Encode) | Issue a fresh code for locked-out guests from your phone; keep a backup code for your cleaner and change it quarterly |
| Smart thermostat | Control temperatures remotely and prevent frozen pipes in winter |

## The Money Backstop

- [ ] Peace-of-mind fund: set aside $500–$1,000 per property for emergencies. Repairs come out of the fund, not out of your cash flow or your sleep.
- [ ] The real backstop is the capex sinking fund: 1–2% of home value a year and at least $10K liquid per property.
